# Returns and refunds

**Changed your mind?** You can cancel your order for any reason up to 14 days after it arrives, as the law provides. We extend this to **30 days** for unopened packs. Email [email] with your order number and we'll send return instructions. You pay the return postage. We refund the price of the goods and the standard delivery charge within 14 days of receiving them back, or of you sending proof of postage, to your original payment method.

**Opened packs.** For hygiene reasons we can't accept change-of-mind returns of packs where the outer seal has been broken after delivery. Individually sealed sachets from an opened multipack can't be returned separately.

**Faulty, damaged or wrong item?** Email [email] with a photo within 30 days and we'll refund or replace it at no cost to you. This doesn't affect your legal rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

**To cancel**, you can write to us in any clear way, or use this form:

> To [Business name, address, email]: I give notice that I cancel my contract of sale of the following goods: [ ]. Ordered on / received on: [ ]. Name: [ ]. Address: [ ]. Date: [ ].
