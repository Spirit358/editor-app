# Safety information

Warmwink steam eye masks warm up when the heat pad meets air. Please read this before your first use.

**How to use.** Open one sachet just before use. Unfold the mask, separate the ear loops along the perforation, close your eyes and hook the loops over your ears. The mask warms to around 40°C and stays warm for about twenty minutes. Remove it when it cools or sooner if you wish.

**Do not use if** you have an eye condition, an eye infection, broken, irritated or inflamed skin around the eyes, or have had recent eye surgery, unless a doctor or optometrist has told you it's fine.

**Ask your doctor first if** you have reduced sensitivity to heat, including from diabetes or circulation problems, or are unsure whether heat on the eyes is suitable for you.

**Always**
- For adults only. Keep out of reach of children and pets.
- Remove contact lenses before use.
- Take the mask off straight away if it feels too hot, uncomfortable, or causes redness or itching.
- Use while resting. Never use while driving or doing anything that needs your eyes.
- Scented masks contain fragrance. Choose unscented if you are sensitive to fragrance.

**Never**
- Never open, cut or tear the heat pad, or swallow its contents. If contents get in the eyes, rinse with plenty of water and seek medical advice. If swallowed, seek medical advice.
- Never microwave, reheat or reuse a mask.
- Never use a mask if the sachet is damaged or was already open.

**Storage and disposal.** Store sealed in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Dispose of the used mask in general waste once it has cooled.

Warmwink is a comfort product, not a medical device.
