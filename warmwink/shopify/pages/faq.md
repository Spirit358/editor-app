# FAQ

**How warm does it get?** Around 40°C, about the temperature of a warm bath. It varies a little with room temperature. If it ever feels too warm, take it off.

**How long does it last?** About twenty minutes.

**Can I wear it with contact lenses or make-up?** Take contact lenses out first. Make-up is fine, though warmth and moisture may smudge it.

**Can I reuse or reheat a mask?** No. Once the heat pad has finished reacting it is spent. Never microwave it.

**Does it treat dry eyes or help me sleep?** Warmwink is a comfort product for relaxing, not a medical device, and we make no medical claims. If you have an eye condition, speak to your optometrist or GP before using heat on your eyes.

**What's in it?** The heat pad contains iron powder, water, activated carbon, vermiculite and salt. Scented masks contain fragrance.

**Can I take them on a plane?** They're flat, light and contain no liquid. Airline rules vary, so check with your airline if unsure.

**How long does delivery take?** [X–Y] working days during launch, tracked. See Delivery.

**Can I return them?** Unopened packs, yes, within 30 days. See Returns.

**How do I dispose of a used mask?** In general waste once it has cooled.
