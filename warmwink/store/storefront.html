Open. Wear. Drift.

The mask starts warming the moment it meets air. Give it a minute before you put it on.

  1. Tear open the sachet

    Unfold the mask and split the ear loops along the perforation. It begins to warm within about a minute.

  2. Hook it over your ears

    Eyes closed, mask on. The soft fabric sits lightly on your face and the warmth builds to around 40°C.

  3. Do nothing for twenty minutes

    When it cools, you're done. It is single-use, so it goes in your general waste bin.

What's inside

The same simple chemistry as a hand warmer, tuned much gentler. Iron powder meets air and slowly oxidises, which gives off warmth. A little water in the pad turns that warmth into a fine, damp heat.

  • Heat pad: iron powder, water, activated carbon, vermiculite, salt
  • Soft non-woven fabric against the skin
  • Stretch ear loops, one size
  • Scented masks contain fragrance; unscented contain none

Questions

How warm does it get?

Around 40°C, about the temperature of a warm bath. It varies a little with room temperature. If it ever feels too warm, take it off.

Can I wear it with contact lenses or make-up?

Take contact lenses out first. Make-up is fine, though the warmth and moisture may smudge it.

Can I reuse or reheat a mask?

No. Once the heat pad has finished reacting it is spent. Never microwave it.

Does it treat dry eyes or help me sleep?

Warmwink is a comfort product for relaxing, not a medical device, and we make no medical claims. If you have an eye condition, speak to your optometrist or GP before using heat on your eyes.

How long does delivery take?

During launch, orders ship from our partner warehouse and arrive in 7–12 working days with tracking. We'll switch to UK next-day dispatch as soon as stock lands.

How do I dispose of it?

The used mask goes in general waste. Check the sachet and box for local recycling guidance.

Use it kindly

Please read before your first wink.

  • For adults. Keep away from children and pets.
  • Do not use on broken, irritated or inflamed skin, or if you have an eye condition or recent eye surgery, without medical advice.
  • Remove contact lenses before use.
  • Take the mask off straight away if it feels too hot or uncomfortable.
  • If you have reduced sensitivity to heat, including from diabetes or circulation problems, ask your doctor first.
  • Do not use while driving or doing anything that needs your eyes.
  • Do not open, cut or microwave the heat pad. Do not swallow the contents.
  • Store sealed, somewhere cool and dry. Use straight after opening.
