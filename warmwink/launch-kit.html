The one thing to know before spending money. Generic 20-packs sell on Amazon UK for £8.59–£16.14 (43p–81p a mask). Branded 5-packs from Spacemasks, Popmask and Sensory Retreats sell for £15–£18.50 (about £3 a mask). The whole margin is the brand. An unbranded dropshipped mask in a plain sachet cannot justify £2 a mask for long, so treat dropshipping as a 3–4 week demand test and order custom-printed boxes as soon as the first 30–50 orders prove people want it.

UK legal checklist

Not legal or tax advice. Official pages (gov.uk, legislation.gov.uk, ASA) could not be opened during research; this is drawn from search extracts of them. Use the links to confirm, and get the VAT point answered by an accountant.

Before the first sale

  • Stay a comfort product. No mention anywhere, including reviews you publish and ads, of dry eye, blepharitis, styes, headaches, migraines, eye strain treatment, insomnia or better sleep. Those claims make it a medical device, which needs MHRA registration. MHRA borderlines guidance
  • You are the "producer". Selling under your own brand makes you legally responsible for the product being safe. GPSR 2005 guidance
  • Get the supplier's safety file: SDS, ingredients, temperature and duration test, skin irritation data, fragrance allergen declaration, batch records.
  • Website must show: your trading name, your own name if a sole trader, a geographic address and an email. E-commerce Regulations, reg 6
  • Consumer terms: total price including delivery, a truthful delivery window, 14-day right to cancel from delivery, refund within 14 days. Unopened boxes are returnable; the hygiene exception only covers packs unsealed after delivery. Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013
  • No fake reviews, fake countdowns or "only 3 left". All compulsory fees in the headline price. The CMA can fine directly. DMCC Act 2024
  • ICO fee: £52 a year (£47 by direct debit). ICO
  • Cookie consent and privacy policy before any ad pixel runs. Email: only people who opted in or bought from you, with an unsubscribe link every time. ICO PECR guidance
  • VAT: ask an accountant this exact question. "I'm a UK sole trader under the £90,000 threshold, selling goods shipped from China direct to UK customers in parcels under £135. Must I register for and charge VAT from my first sale, and who is the importer?" Sources disagree for UK-based sellers. Stock held in a UK warehouse follows the normal £90,000 threshold. HMRC guidance

Within 30 days

  • Product liability insurance. Not compulsory but you carry the liability; entry quotes are advertised around £60–80 a year, likely more for a heat product used on the eyes. Tell the insurer what it is and where it's made.
  • Tell HMRC you're self-employed: register for Self Assessment by 5 October 2027 once trading income passes £1,000. gov.uk
  • Creators you gift product to must label posts "Ad" up front; "gifted" alone isn't enough, and you share responsibility. ASA influencer guide
  • Keep a complaints log and note which supplier batch each order came from.

Before your own-brand box

  • Box and sachet carry: brand, UK name and address, batch number, instructions and the warnings on the Safety page.
  • Independent UK lab test of temperature profile and leakage; ask Trading Standards or a product safety consultant to confirm classification and warnings.
  • Check the commodity code and duty rate on the UK Trade Tariff before a bulk import.
  • If you add skin claims (puffiness, dark circles) the product may become a cosmetic with its own rules. Don't.

What you can and can't say

Say

  • relaxing, soothing warmth, gentle steam
  • helps you unwind; a moment of calm before bed
  • a break from screens
  • lavender scented
  • "warms to about 40°C for about 20 minutes", once you hold the supplier's test report

Don't say

  • treats, relieves or helps dry eye, styes, blepharitis
  • relieves headaches or migraines
  • improves sleep, promotes restful sleep, helps insomnia
  • reduces eye strain, puffiness or dark circles
  • clinically proven, doctor recommended
  • Japanese, or made in Japan, unless it is
